Switch

Unit 03 · Cisco Business Managed

The Cisco Business managed switch is the distribution layer of the lab — the central point that every other wired device connects back to. Unlike an unmanaged consumer switch that simply forwards all traffic to all ports, a managed switch gives me granular control over how traffic flows. VLANs, port-based access control, traffic monitoring, and link aggregation are all on the table when the time comes to segment the network further.

Choosing a Cisco Business switch was a deliberate decision. Cisco is the standard in enterprise networking, and working with its management interface at home directly translates to skills I use professionally. Every configuration I practice here is one less thing I have to learn on the job.
