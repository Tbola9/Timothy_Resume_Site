Access Point

Unit 04 · Primary Wireless

The access point handles all wireless connectivity in the lab environment. Every wireless device on the network — phones, laptops, tablets, and any other Wi-Fi clients — connects through here before reaching the switch and the rest of the infrastructure. Keeping the access point as a dedicated device rather than relying on a combined router/AP gives me the flexibility to manage wired and wireless traffic independently and upgrade either without touching the other.

As the lab matures and the Protectli comes online, the access point will be segmented onto its own VLAN — isolating wireless clients from the core wired infrastructure. That separation is a basic but important security principle: devices that do not need to talk to each other should not be able to.
