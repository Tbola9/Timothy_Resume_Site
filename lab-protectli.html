Firewall

Unit 02 · Protectli V1410

The Protectli V1410 is the device I am most looking forward to adding to the rack. It is a purpose-built firewall appliance with 2.5GbE ports, running OPNsense — an open-source firewall and routing platform that gives me full control over everything entering and leaving the network. Where the ISP modem is a device I borrow, this is one I own completely.

OPNsense handles routing between my WAN and LAN interfaces, enforces firewall rules, and runs OpenVPN for secure remote access back into the lab from anywhere. Beyond that, OPNsense supports IDS/IPS through Suricata or Zenarmor, giving me the ability to inspect traffic and detect threats at the network edge — the same class of capability you would find in an enterprise environment. This device is the foundation that everything else in the rack will eventually depend on for security and segmentation.
