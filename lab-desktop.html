Desktop Workstation

Unit 06 · Lab Management Interface

The desktop workstation is the primary interface for managing everything else in the rack. From here I access the management interfaces of every other device — the switch console, the hypervisor dashboard, firewall rules, and any VMs running on the lab. It is the control plane in human terms: the place where decisions get made and configurations get applied.

Having a dedicated management workstation rather than using a personal laptop keeps lab traffic on a known, consistent endpoint. It also means the management plane is always available even when other devices are being reconfigured, rebooted, or tested. In an enterprise environment this concept maps directly to out-of-band management — you always want a way back in regardless of what is happening on the network.
