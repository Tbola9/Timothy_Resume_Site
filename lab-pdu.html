Power Distribution Unit

Unit 07 · Networked Power Management

The PDU is the last unit in the rack and arguably one of the most practical. It distributes power to every other device in the lab — modem, firewall, switch, hypervisor, workstation, and access point — from a single managed unit. What makes it more than a power strip is that it is networked into the switch, which means I can monitor power consumption per outlet and remotely force a hard restart on any device by cycling its outlet without physically touching the rack.

Remote power cycling is a capability that sounds simple but solves a real problem: when a device stops responding to its own management interface, the only option left is a hard reboot. With a networked PDU that becomes a software action instead of a physical one. In a production environment this is standard — in a home lab it is the difference between a quick fix and a trip to the hardware.
