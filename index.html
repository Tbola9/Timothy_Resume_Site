The Lab

My home network begins at the edge with a Protectli V1410 running OPNsense, handling routing, firewall, VPN, and IDS/IPS. From there the network flows downstream to a Cisco managed switch, a Proxmox hypervisor, a dedicated workstation, and a Velop access point.

This isn't simply a hobby network. It's a working lab built around the same discipline I bring to any production environment — the place where I learn, break things on purpose, and test changes before they ever touch anything that matters.
