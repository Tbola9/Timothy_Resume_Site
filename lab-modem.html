ISP Modem

Unit 01 · 500 Mb Residential Circuit

Every network starts somewhere, and mine starts here. The ISP modem is the device provided by my internet service provider that bridges the public internet to my private network. Currently supporting a 500 Mb residential circuit, it handles the handoff between the ISP infrastructure and everything downstream. It is a capable device for now, but it is on the replacement list for 2026 as I continue to build out the lab toward a more purpose-built edge setup.

This device is one of the few in the rack I do not fully control — the ISP owns and manages it. That is exactly why it is the first thing to be replaced. Owning the edge means owning the first line of decisions about what comes in and what goes out.
